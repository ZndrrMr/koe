# Sakura Sakura prototype audio

`sakura-sakura.mp3` is an MP3 transcode of **Sakura Sakura.song.ogg**, a
performance by Kanohara published on Wikimedia Commons under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported license.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sakura_Sakura.song.ogg
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Changes: Wikimedia's 2026 MP3 transcode is bundled unchanged.

`line-reference.m4a` is the 0.88–5.20 second opening excerpt, transcoded to AAC.
`sample-attempt.m4a` is that excerpt with a small timing and level adjustment
for deterministic pronunciation-pipeline verification. Those adaptations are
also offered under CC BY-SA 3.0.

The traditional composition and lyrics are identified by the source as public
domain. The prototype deliberately displays only the selected opening line.
