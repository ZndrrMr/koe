Koe / 声 · visual system study · ZAN-909

The voice belongs in the margin.

Product subject

A calm, immediate place for a learner to speak Japanese, hear Koe answer, notice one useful improvement, and keep talking.

The system borrows the primary book reference’s editorial paper, blue engraving, fine rules, high-contrast serif, and singular ochre emphasis. It does not borrow its library navigation or turn Koe into a reading app.

01 · source reading

What the full corpus permits

All sixteen saved references were reviewed. The first ten define editorial structure and phone-scale craft; the final six prove that a single image with confident negative space can carry a composition. Reference 008 remains the authority whenever the corpus disagrees.

Keep

Paper warmth, a single illustration or photograph, asymmetrical editorial hierarchy, fine rules, quiet blue, one ochre mark.

Translate

Book plate → voice plate. Quote → current conversational proposition. Chapter metadata → truthful voice-state label.

Limit

Japanese lettering appears only where it names Koe, a state, or spoken content. It is language, never pattern.

Reject

Dashboard tiles, generic AI glow, stacked chat bubbles, capsule fields, ornamental seals, scenic spectacle, and decorative motion.

02 · directions at 390 × 844 pt

Three coherent readings

Each direction uses the same product truth and the same exact iPhone canvas. The primary reference is shown at the same width beside each pair so hierarchy, image share, type scale, and density can be judged directly.

Direction A · selected

Blue Marginalia / 声の余白

Koe behaves like an annotated folio: a small blue voice plate, generous cream paper, a large serif proposition, and one useful ochre emphasis. The live conversation replaces a decorative waveform with six static, legible engraving states.

Why this wins

It is closest to 008 without imitating its book UI. It gives Koe an ownable subject-specific signature—the voice plate—while leaving spoken Japanese, state truth, and the next action unmistakable.

Direction B · credible, not selected

Conversation Ticket / ことばの札

A structured ticket system drawn from 004, 005, and 006: sage and ochre fields, indexed metadata, and a framed specimen-like voice plate.

Why it loses

It is useful and legible, but its labeled compartments make a fluid conversation feel administered. At live scale, Koe starts to resemble an instrument panel and drifts away from 008’s open, quote-led calm.

Direction C · credible, not selected

Night Reading / 夜の声

An intimate indigo edition drawn from 002, 011, and 015. Pale type and a single ochre voice disc turn conversation into a focused nighttime encounter.

Why it loses

It gives Koe atmosphere, but dark spectacle becomes the first impression and weakens the paper/engraving fidelity Zander chose. Keep this only as the selected system’s dark appearance, with the same layout and no new scenic metaphor.

Conversation state grammar

State is encoded three ways: a distinct static plate form, a plain-language label, and the primary action’s label. Color is supplemental. Transitions may crossfade for 120–180 ms; Reduce Motion switches instantly. No plate runs an idle loop.

Image and plate placement

Fresh microphone education

310×240 pt blue engraving at top of content: two offset voice contours meeting, with generous negative space. One screen only if permission is not yet resolved. Live copy and button beneath.

Home / start

310×248 pt plate, centered, 24 pt after header; 28–33% of safe canvas. The bottom 35% of the art must remain quiet so the title never overlaps detail.

Listening

204×204 pt code-native state plate centered in a 300×300 pt stage. Generated art is absent; state must react instantly and remain legible without image loading.

Understanding / tutor speaking

Same 204×204 pt stage and anchor. Swap only the static plate form and label. Speaking may respond to measured energy at ≤10 Hz only if ZAN-912 proves the cost; otherwise static.

Compact feedback

No hero art. One ruled note beneath the current exchange; ochre underlines only the changed mora/word. Reference playback and Retry remain code-native.

Recoverable error

No sentimental image. Broken blue/error plate at 144×144 pt, explicit cause, one recovery action, one safe exit. Never communicate failure by color alone.

Ending

Reuse the home plate at 120×96 pt above a plain confirmation. Keep talking and Finish session are separate full-width rule regions with ≥52 pt height.

Ended / coda

280×180 pt blue engraving with up to three abstract voice fragments arranged like marginal notes. If there are no saved moments, use only type and rules.

Dark appearance

Same crops and layout. Recolor line art to #A9C6D5 on #111B25; ochre becomes #D8B75C. Do not generate a second scenic family or invert raster photographs automatically.

Production art rules for ZAN-910

Family

Single-color cobalt intaglio/wood-engraving linework on warm uncoated paper. Visible hatch irregularity, 1–2 pt apparent line weight at 390 pt screen width, no photorealism, no faux antique damage.

Subject

Voice as exchange: two contours, folded speech, open page-like planes, or a thread passing between marks. Japanese speech rhythm may inform spacing; avoid torii, waves, red sun, cherry blossoms, seals, geisha, samurai, and calligraphy props.

Export

Transparent WebP/PNG where possible, 3× source, Display-P3 reviewed then exported sRGB. Home/onboarding/coda master 1200×960 px (5:4); recovery 600×600 px. No baked copy, device frames, shadows, gradients, or UI.

Crop

Keep the focal form within the central 62% width. Preserve 18% clear space on every edge and 35% calm lower field for home/onboarding. Cropping may remove peripheral hatch, never the voice exchange.

Accessibility role

Home/onboarding images are meaningful and receive concise labels. Live state plates receive the current state as value. Coda art is decorative if its saved moments are already live text. Recovery art never substitutes for error text.

Acceptance

Composite every candidate into the exact 390×844 and smallest supported iPhone canvases. Reject illegible hatching, accidental letterforms, embedded text, inconsistent ink, crowded crops, or imagery that becomes the action.

04 · accessibility and exclusions

Calm cannot depend on smallness

Contrast and color

All normal text pairs meet 4.5:1; primary text and actions target 7:1. Ochre is only an emphasis ground. Interactive boundaries use Rule Strong, not Hairline.

Reading order

State label → state explanation → current utterance → optional note → primary action. The plate is adjacent to its state and never interrupts VoiceOver order.

Targets and safe areas

All controls own their final visible region and are at least 44×44 pt. Major actions are ≥52 pt; primary listening action is 64–76 pt. Keep 12 pt beyond resolved top/bottom insets.

Motion and sound

Every phase is understandable muted and static. Announce actual phase changes politely; do not announce energy updates. Haptics and audio support state but never carry it alone.

  • No generic AI object.The voice plate is editorial linework tied to a named real phase, not a luminous orb, soft gradient blob, or ambient decorative waveform.
  • No dashboard cards.Rules divide semantic regions; they do not wrap every paragraph, metric, history item, or setting into containers.
  • No ornamental Japan.声 names Koe. Japanese labels name real states. Cultural motifs do not substitute for product meaning.
  • No motion-led spectacle.No perpetual breathing, rotating, shimmering, floating, or particle fields. State transitions are brief and optional.
  • No chat transcript as the stage.Show the current exchange and at most one useful note. Full transcript/history remains out of the minimal core.
  • No theme sprawl.One light paper system and its faithful dark appearance. No per-state palette, persona skins, or scenic environments.
  • No tiny editorial affectation.Captions may be small only when supplemental. Instructions, actions, transcript, and errors use accessible body sizes.
  • No generated UI copy.Artwork contains no text, button, label, device chrome, or false acoustic data. All meaning remains code-native and localizable.
05 · downstream handoff

Exact implementation boundary

ZAN-910 · image family

Generate only onboarding, home, recovery, and coda art from the placement rules above. Do not generate live phase plates; those are deterministic vectors. Attach in-device composites before accepting exports.

ZAN-911 · core screens

Implement Blue Marginalia across fresh permission education, home, live phases, compact feedback, recovery, ending, and coda. Preserve the screen map and remove unlisted surfaces.

ZAN-912 · state motion

Begin with the six static plate forms. Only add a 120–180 ms crossfade or measured-energy response after proving it does not delay voice, navigation, transcript, or playback work.

ZAN-913–915 · verification

Treat type scale, image share/crop, state triple-coding, whole-region actions, safe-area spacing, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver order, Reduce Motion, and light/dark equivalence as screenshot/runtime gates.

Deliberately excluded from this design contract: preferences unless product scope later proves one is required; conversation history; personas, lessons, goals, streaks, and modes; marketing hero imagery; handwriting and music surfaces; illustration embedded in feedback; and any redesign implementation inside ZAN-909.
